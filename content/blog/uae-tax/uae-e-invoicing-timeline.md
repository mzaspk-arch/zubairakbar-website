## Key takeaways

- **Ministerial Decision No. 243 of 2025** sets the scope; **No. 244 of 2025** sets the phased dates.
- Revenue of **AED 50 million or more**: appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) by **31 July 2026**, go live **1 January 2027**.
- Revenue **below AED 50 million**: appoint an ASP by **31 March 2027**, go live **1 July 2027**.
- **B2C** transactions are excluded for now.

</div>

## The timeline

| Who | Appoint an ASP by | Go live |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot participants and voluntary adopters | — | from 1 July 2026 |
| Revenue AED 50 million or more | 31 July 2026 | 1 January 2027 |
| Revenue below AED 50 million | 31 March 2027 | 1 July 2027 |
| Government entities | 31 March 2027 | 1 October 2027 |

If your revenue is AED 50 million or more and you have not yet appointed an ASP, you are already behind the 31 July 2026 deadline. Speak to a provider now.

## Who is in scope

Any person doing business in the UAE is in scope, with some exclusions, including:

- government entities acting in a sovereign capacity;
- international passenger air transport and related electronic tickets;
- international air cargo with airway bills (for 24 months);
- VAT-exempt or zero-rated financial services.

Businesses that only sell to consumers (B2C) are excluded until a future date set by the Minister.

## What changes in practice

- Invoices are no longer PDFs emailed to customers. They are **structured data** sent through your ASP to your customer's ASP and reported to the FTA.
- E-invoices and credit notes must be issued and sent **within 14 days** of the transaction.
- Your customer master data — names, TRNs, addresses — has to be clean, because the system checks it.

## Your preparation checklist

1. **Confirm your phase.** Base it on your annual revenue.
2. **Appoint an ASP** before your deadline and check it connects to your ERP or accounting system.
3. **Clean your master data.** Verify every customer and supplier TRN, legal name and address.
4. **Map your invoice fields** to the e-invoice data standard with your ASP.
5. **Review credit-note and invoicing workflows** so documents go out within 14 days.
6. **Test with real transactions** before go-live, then keep a fallback process for system outages.
