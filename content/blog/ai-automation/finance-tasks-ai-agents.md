## Key takeaways

- AI is already reliable for **reading documents, matching transactions and drafting** routine work.
- The best results come from **AI preparing, people approving**.
- Start with one high-volume, rules-based task and measure the time saved.
- Keep **an audit trail** of what the AI did and who approved it.

</div>

## Where to start

Good candidates for automation share three traits: high volume, clear rules and a person who can check the output quickly. These five fit that pattern in most finance teams.

## 1. Supplier invoice capture

An AI agent reads supplier invoices from email or uploads, extracts the supplier, TRN, dates, amounts and VAT, and creates a draft bill in your accounting system.

**Human check:** approve drafts, especially new suppliers and invoices with unusual VAT treatment.

## 2. Bank reconciliation

The agent matches bank lines to invoices, payments and receipts, including partial and grouped payments, and proposes matches with a confidence score.

**Human check:** review low-confidence matches and anything unmatched at month end.

## 3. Expense coding

Receipts and card transactions are coded to the right account, cost centre and VAT treatment based on past patterns and your policy.

**Human check:** spot-check samples and review anything outside policy.

## 4. Collections follow-up

The agent reviews overdue invoices, drafts reminders in the right tone for each customer, and schedules follow-ups.

**Human check:** approve messages before they go to key customers or before any escalation.

## 5. Management reporting

The agent pulls the trial balance, compares it with budget and the prior period, and drafts commentary explaining the main variances.

**Human check:** the finance lead edits the commentary and signs off the pack.

## Where people must stay in the loop

Keep human approval for anything that:

- moves money (payments, refunds, write-offs);
- is submitted to a regulator or tax authority;
- changes master data such as bank details;
- goes to customers, banks or auditors.

## How to roll it out

1. **Pick one task** and measure how long it takes today.
2. **Run the AI alongside the current process** for a month and compare.
3. **Set approval rules** and log every action the AI takes.
4. **Expand only when the error rate is acceptable** and the team trusts the output.
